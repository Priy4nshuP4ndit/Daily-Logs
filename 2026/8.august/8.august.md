## 🧠 Key Takeaways

* Reconnaissance is one of the earliest phases of a security assessment.
* **Passive reconnaissance** relies on publicly available information without directly interacting with the target's systems.
* OSINT can reveal surprisingly large amounts of information from seemingly harmless public sources.
* Different tools specialize in different types of information gathering.
* Understanding the information itself is more important than simply knowing how to run a tool.
* Reconnaissance should always be performed only on systems and targets where I have permission to investigate.
