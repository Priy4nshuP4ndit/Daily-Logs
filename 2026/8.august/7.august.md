## Key Understanding

A network is not simply "the Internet." It is a collection of interconnected devices and networks that move data using addressing and forwarding mechanisms.

I also started developing the mental model that networking concepts are connected: IP addressing, DNS, routers, switches, private/public IPs, NAT and routing are not isolated topics; each solves a specific problem in the journey of a packet.
